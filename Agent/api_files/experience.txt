Surgery Prep Room — Precise surface placement and instrument layout

A surgery preparation room with strict focus on sterile fields and
precise instrument layout. Stainless-steel instrument trays rest on top
of wheeled surgery carts; sterilizing equipment (an autoclave, a
sterilizer drum) sits along the back wall. A surgeon and a scrub nurse
are present, going through final pre-op checks. Lighting is bright
surgical white. The room reads as meticulously organized — every tool
has a place on a tray or cart surface, never the floor.

[interactions]
Touching any small instrument (scalpel, forceps, syringe) should
highlight the instrument and make the surgeon comment on its specific
use ("Forceps — for grasping tissue", "Scalpel — sharp, handle
carefully"). Touching the autoclave or sterilizer should make the
surgeon briefly explain the sterilization status of the load inside.
Touching the surgeon should make him ask the scrub nurse to ready a
specific instrument; that instrument briefly highlights yellow on its
tray.

A short story arc: touching the surgeon → he asks for forceps and
Forceps_01 highlights. Touching the highlighted forceps → the scrub
nurse walks over to the central cart and acknowledges. Touching an
instrument tray on the central cart afterward → the surgical lighting
brightens and the surgeon nods "ready to begin".
