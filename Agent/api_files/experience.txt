CT Examination Room (compact, clean, realistic)

Create a believable CT examination room that reads as a real, enclosed space (not a sparse cluster of props).
Room size approx 6m x 6m with 4 walls, a ceiling, and a floor. Keep a clear walking lane around the scanner and table, but avoid big empty gaps.

Primary anchor and workflow:
- Tomograph/CT scanner centered as the focal point (near the middle of the room).
- Table aligned with the scanner bore (in/out axis).
- Patient present and walking around the room.

Control corner (right side preferred):
- X-Ray Monitor (dual screen) at the head of the table, slightly to the right.
- Chair near the monitor/control station.
- Utility Cart near the table on the monitor side.

Clinical support:
- IV Drip near the head of the table, opposite the monitor.
- Waste Bucket near the chair or by the entry corner.
- Sanitizer mounted near the entry wall.
- Clock mounted high on the wall above or near the monitor.
- Caution Sign placed near the scanner, slightly in front.

Realism requirements:
- Include at least 10 total interior objects (scanner, table, monitors, chair, cart, IV, bucket, sanitizer, clock, sign, patient).

Room exterior (required):
- 4 walls forming a 6m x 6m enclosure (wall height 3m).
- Front wall must have a doorway (approx 1.2m wide, centered at x=0) so the room can be entered/exited.
- Ceiling covering the full room.
- One ceiling light fixture mounted at the center of the ceiling, with bright hospital lighting (SetLightIntensity).
