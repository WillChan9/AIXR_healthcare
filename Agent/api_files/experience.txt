CT Examination Room (compact, clean, efficient)

This is a small, functional CT room (approx. 6m x 6m) with a clean, minimal layout. The CT scanner is the anchor and everything is placed close to it for a tight clinical workflow. Keep the layout compact (no big gaps), with a clear walking lane around the scanner and table.

Placement guidance:
- Tomograph: centered as the focal point (near the middle of the room).
- Table: aligned with the scanner bore (in/out axis of the Tomograph).
- X-Ray Monitor (dual screen): at the head of the table, slightly to one side (right side preferred).
- Chair: near the monitor/control station.
- IV Drip: near the head of the table, opposite the monitor.
- Utility Cart: near the table on the side with the monitor.
- Waste Bucket: near the chair or by the entry corner.
- Sanitizer: mounted near the entry wall.
- Clock: mounted high on the wall above or near the monitor.
- Caution Sign: placed near the scanner, slightly in front.
