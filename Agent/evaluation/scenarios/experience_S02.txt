Hospital Ward (4-Bed) — Symmetric multi-bed patient care room

A spacious, well-organized hospital ward holding four patient beds
arranged in two symmetric pairs along the long walls. Each bed has its
own bedside table and a chair for visitors, with wide walkways between
beds for nurse access. Two nurses move between beds tending to patients;
two of the four beds are occupied by patients, the other two are empty
and made up. The room should feel calm and routine — soft warm lighting,
neat rows, easy circulation.

[interactions]
When the player walks up to either nurse, the nurse explains what she's
currently doing on rounds (e.g., "checking the IV", "updating the
chart"). Touching either occupied bed lets the patient briefly describe
how they feel. Touching a chart on a bedside table should briefly
highlight the corresponding patient bed, and the nurse should comment on
that patient's progress.

A short story arc: touching one nurse should make her walk to the
nearest occupied bed and start a check-up animation. Touching the
patient afterward should make the patient say a brief positive line
about feeling better. Touching the bedside chart finally should brighten
the lighting slightly as the nurse marks a note.
