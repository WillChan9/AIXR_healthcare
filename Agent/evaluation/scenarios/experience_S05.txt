Dental Office — Single-chair specialty clinic with focused tooling

A bright, clean dental treatment room organized around one centrally-placed
dentist chair under a focused overhead lamp. Beside the chair sits a wheeled
dental equipment cart loaded with the dentist's hand-tools (mirrors, picks,
syringes, scalers); a wall-mounted dental X-ray arm reaches over the chair
from one side. A small adjustable stool for the dentist sits on the other
side of the chair, and a sink with a towel dispenser is set against the
wall. A dentist (Doctor) stands by the chair preparing for an exam; a
patient waits seated. The atmosphere should feel clinical, sterile, and
focused — minimal clutter beyond the working zone.

[interactions]
When the player touches the dentist, the dentist greets the player and
explains today's procedure ("a routine cleaning and check-up"). When the
player touches the dental X-ray arm, it should briefly highlight and the
dentist should explain that it is positioned to image specific teeth.
Touching any of the small instruments on the equipment cart (dental
mirror, explorer, syringe) should display a short description of the
tool's purpose ("Dental mirror — for indirect viewing of back teeth"
etc.). Touching the patient should make him say a brief line about
where his tooth aches.

A short story arc: when the player first touches the dentist, the
dental X-ray arm briefly highlights to indicate it's needed for the
exam. When the player then touches the X-ray arm, the dentist walks
one step toward the chair and instructs the patient to "open wide".
When the player finally touches the patient, the overhead light
brightens slightly to signal "examination underway" and the dentist
plays a Talking animation acknowledging the start of the procedure.
