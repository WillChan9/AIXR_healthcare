Cosmetic Treatment Room — Aesthetic clinic with laser-based devices

A medium-sized cosmetic / aesthetic treatment room arranged around a
single reclining cosmetic chair where the patient relaxes. A large
cosmetic laser device stands on one side of the chair; a smaller
secondary laser device and a multi-function cosmetic combine machine
sit on the other side. A wheeled cosmetic cart holds smaller bottles,
applicators, and skincare supplies within easy reach of the chair.
A cosmetic technician (Doctor) operates the equipment; the patient
reclines in the chair. The room should feel modern, calm, and
slightly dim with focused accent lighting on the chair area, more
spa than hospital — a private, indulgent atmosphere.

[interactions]
When the player touches the cosmetic technician, she explains today's
treatment ("laser hair removal", "skin resurfacing"). Touching either
cosmetic laser device should briefly highlight it and display a
description ("Cosmetic laser — non-ablative resurfacing"). Touching
the cosmetic combine machine should describe its multi-function uses
(microdermabrasion, ultrasound, etc.). Touching the cosmetic cart
should highlight the bottles on top of it briefly. Touching the
patient should make her describe how she feels ("a little warm where
the laser passes").

A short story arc: when the player first touches the technician, the
main cosmetic laser briefly highlights yellow to indicate it's the
device for today's session. When the player then touches the
highlighted laser, the technician walks one step closer to the
patient and says "starting low intensity now". When the player
finally touches the patient, the lighting dims slightly to "treatment
mode" and the technician plays a Talking animation announcing the
session has begun.
