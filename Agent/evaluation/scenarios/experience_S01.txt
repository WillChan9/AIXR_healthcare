Emergency Triage Room — Small cramped space for rapid patient assessment

A small, cramped emergency triage room used for rapid patient assessment
under time pressure. The space is tightly packed with essential emergency
equipment: a stretcher against the back wall, an exam table flanked by a
wheeled supply trolley, and a wall-mounted patient monitor. One EMT works
urgently near the stretcher; one patient lies on the exam table being
assessed. Lighting should be bright and cool — the atmosphere reads as
functional, urgent, and minimal-empty-space.

[interactions]
When the player approaches and touches the EMT, the EMT briefly explains
the patient's symptoms and gestures toward the monitor. When the player
touches the patient monitor, the EMT reads out the patient's vitals
(heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen saturation). When the player walks
up to the patient, the patient softly describes where the pain is.
Touching the supply trolley should briefly highlight the small props
(syringe, stethoscope) on top of it.

A short story arc: when the player first touches the EMT, the monitor
briefly highlights to show vitals. When the player then touches the
monitor, the EMT walks one step toward the patient and states an urgent
observation. When the player finally touches the patient, the lighting
brightens slightly to signal "examination underway".
