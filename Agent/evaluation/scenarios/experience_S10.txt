Nurse Station (Open) — Maximum scale and density stress test

A large open nurse station serving as the central hub of a busy
hospital floor. Multiple desks with monitors and computers face the
open floor; supply cabinets and medication carts line the perimeter
walls; three nurses work at different stations doing different tasks
(one charting at a desk, one organizing medications at a cart, one
fielding patient call lights). The atmosphere is busy and bright with
constant low-level activity — this is the operations center of the
ward.

[interactions]
Touching any nurse should make her say what task she's currently doing
(different per nurse — e.g., "updating patient charts", "preparing
medications", "answering call light from room 12"). Touching any desk
computer should briefly display "active patient list — 18 admitted".
Touching any medication cart should describe the contents (e.g.,
"morning medication round — 12 doses prepared"). Touching any supply
cabinet should highlight it briefly.

A short story arc: touching the first (charting) nurse → she explains
the unit's current census and a specific medication cart highlights.
Touching that cart afterward → the second nurse walks over to it and
updates the inventory. Touching her in turn → the room lighting subtly
shifts to indicate "shift change", and the third nurse takes over the
charting desk while the second starts a round.
