Ophthalmology Exam Room — Specialty 1-on-1 eye examination

A focused ophthalmology examination room arranged for a single patient
seated in a specialized ophthalmology chair facing a slit-lamp / vision
testing setup. The room contains an autorefkeratometer (eye-refraction
machine), a colposcope-style imaging device, a small ophthalmology
table with reference glasses and a skin microscope, and an eye chart
poster on the wall opposite the patient. The ophthalmologist (Doctor)
sits on a small adjustable stool facing the patient. Lighting should
be slightly dim (to support pupil dilation tests) with one focused
lamp. The room reads as quiet, clinical, and focused on a single
seated patient.

[interactions]
When the player touches the ophthalmologist, she explains what test
is currently in progress ("checking refraction", "pupil dilation
review"). Touching the autorefkeratometer should display its function
("Autorefkeratometer — measures the eye's refraction automatically").
Touching the colposcope-style imaging device should describe its use.
Touching the eye chart poster should display "Snellen chart — tests
visual acuity at 20 feet". Touching the patient should make him
describe what he can/cannot see clearly.

A short story arc: when the player first touches the ophthalmologist,
the autorefkeratometer briefly highlights yellow to indicate the
current test. When the player then touches the highlighted machine,
the doctor walks one step closer to the patient and says "small
pressure now". When the player finally touches the patient, the
overhead light brightens momentarily to signal "examination complete"
and the doctor plays a Talking animation summarizing the result.
