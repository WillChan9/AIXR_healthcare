Clinical Laboratory — Bench-dense diagnostic lab

A working clinical laboratory laid out around long bench tables that
run along multiple walls. Heavy diagnostic instruments line the
benches in functional clusters: a centrifuge and biochemical analyzer
in one bench cluster, a hematology analyzer and immunoanalyzer in
another, and microscopes (general + specimen) at the far bench. A
test tube rack and petri dish sit on a central bench surrounded by
flasks for active samples. A lab doctor (Doctor) is reviewing results
at one bench; a lab technician (Nurse) is operating the centrifuge.
The room should feel busy but organized — every bench is occupied by
specific equipment with no empty space, no patients (this is a
back-of-house lab, not a clinical exam room). Lighting is bright
clinical white.

[interactions]
When the player touches the lab doctor, he explains what tests are
currently running ("running CBC", "checking electrolytes"). Touching
any analyzer (biochemical, hematology, immuno, cellular) should
display a one-sentence description of what it tests for ("Hematology
analyzer — measures complete blood counts"). Touching either
microscope should describe its specialty (general histology vs skin
microscopy). Touching the test tube rack or petri dish should mention
the samples currently in process. Touching the technician should
make her say what step of the workflow she is on.

A short story arc: when the player first touches the lab doctor, the
centrifuge briefly highlights to indicate it is the current focus.
When the player then touches the highlighted centrifuge, the
technician walks one step toward it to retrieve a sample. When the
player finally touches a microscope, the lighting brightens slightly
and the doctor plays a Talking animation announcing "results ready
for review".
