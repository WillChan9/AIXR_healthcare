Radiology Imaging Suite — Large-machine scan room with separate control area

A spacious radiology imaging suite zoned into two distinct areas: a
large scan-equipment zone dominated by big machines (a CT-style
tomograph, a mammograph, an ultrasound machine, and additional X-ray
imaging hardware), and a separate control / monitoring zone along one
wall containing a control panel, multiple monitors for image review,
and a chair for the technician. A radiologist (Doctor) operates the
control panel; a patient lies prepared on the imaging table inside the
scan zone. A nurse assists patient positioning. Lighting should be
clinical white, slightly cooler than typical wards. The room should
read as "scan zone vs control zone" with a clear visual separation
between the heavy machinery and the control desk.

[interactions]
When the player touches the radiologist at the control panel, the
radiologist explains what scan is being performed ("CT scan of the
abdomen" or similar). Touching any of the big imaging machines
(tomograph, mammograph, ultrasound) should display a one-sentence
description of what it does. Touching the control panel should make
the radiologist say "preparing the scan now" and a nearby monitor
should briefly highlight. Touching the nurse should make her describe
the patient prep ("contrast injected, ready in two minutes").
Touching the patient should make him describe how he feels ("a little
nervous about the scan").

A short story arc: when the player first touches the radiologist, the
main scanning machine highlights yellow to indicate it's the active
scanner. When the player then touches the highlighted machine, the
nurse walks one step toward the patient table to assist positioning.
When the player finally touches the patient, the lighting dims
slightly and the radiologist plays a Talking animation announcing
"scan beginning".
