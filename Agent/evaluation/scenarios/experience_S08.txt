Rehabilitation Corridor — Elongated room, linear layout

A long, narrow rehabilitation corridor used for patient walking
exercises. Handrails run along both long walls at hand height for
support, and mobility aids — a wheelchair and a walker — sit at one
end of the corridor. A physical therapist stands roughly midway down
the corridor, watching for patient progress. The layout is strictly
linear along the long axis; everything is arranged to guide the player
from one end to the other.

[interactions]
Touching the therapist should make her explain today's exercise routine
and gesture toward the far end of the corridor. Touching the wheelchair
or walker should describe its use ("wheelchair — for non-ambulatory
patients", "walker — for patients regaining strength"). Touching either
handrail should produce a short "lean here for stability" prompt. As
the player walks the length of the corridor, the therapist should follow
along with a few steps in their direction.

A short story arc: touching the therapist → she gestures toward the
mobility-aids end of the corridor (the wheelchair highlights). Touching
the wheelchair → the therapist walks halfway down the corridor toward
the player. Reaching and touching the handrail at the far end →
lighting brightens briefly and the therapist congratulates the
player's progress.
