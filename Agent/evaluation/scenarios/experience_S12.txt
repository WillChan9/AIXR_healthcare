Maternity / Neonatal Ward — Multi-bassinette nursery with infant care

A warm, well-lit neonatal ward dedicated to newborn care. Three
bassinettes are arranged in a gentle row along one wall, with a
shared changing table at one end and an infant warmer with
phototherapy lamp at the other. A pediatric exam table sits in the
center of the room for routine checks, with a small infant scale
beside it. The walls are decorated with soft posters; lighting is
warm and indirect to avoid disturbing the newborns. Two nurses
attend the ward — one is checking on the bassinettes, the other is
weighing a baby on the scale. The atmosphere should feel calm and
nurturing, with multiple small pieces of furniture rather than a
few big ones.

[interactions]
When the player touches either nurse, the nurse softly explains what
she is doing ("checking temperatures", "weighing for the morning
record"). Touching any bassinette should briefly highlight it and
make the nearer nurse say a short comforting line ("baby's sleeping
peacefully", "feeding time soon"). Touching the infant warmer should
display its function ("Infant warmer with phototherapy — used to
treat jaundice"). Touching the changing table or pediatric exam table
should make a nurse comment that it's prepared for the next infant
check.

A short story arc: when the player first touches one of the nurses,
the infant warmer at the end of the row briefly highlights to indicate
it's currently in use. When the player then touches the highlighted
warmer, the second nurse walks one step toward it to adjust a setting.
When the player finally touches a bassinette, the lighting brightens
slightly to signal "morning rounds underway" and both nurses play a
brief Talking animation acknowledging the routine has begun.
