Doctor's Office — Functional zone testing (desk zone + exam zone)

A doctor's office cleanly divided into two functional zones: a desk
and office area on one side (with a computer, paper folders for patient
records, and an office chair) and an examination area on the other side
(with an exam table, a small adjustable stool, and basic medical
equipment like a stethoscope and folder for taking notes). A doctor
sits at the desk reviewing a patient's chart. The two zones should read
as visually separate but in the same continuous room — desk for
paperwork on one side, exam table for clinical work on the other.

[interactions]
Touching the doctor at his desk should make him greet the player and
gesture toward the exam table ("please have a seat, I'll be right
over"). Touching the computer should briefly display "patient records
— Mr. Johnson, 42 yrs". Touching any folder on the desk should show
what kind of records are inside (lab results, history, imaging
reports). Touching the exam table should make the doctor stand up from
the desk.

A short story arc: touching the doctor → he stays seated and points
toward the exam table (which highlights briefly). Touching the
highlighted exam table → the doctor walks from his desk over to the
exam table. Touching a small piece of medical equipment on the exam
side afterward → the doctor explains the next step of the examination.
