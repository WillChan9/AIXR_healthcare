Physical Therapy Gym — High-diversity interaction test room

A physical therapy gym designed for active movement rehabilitation. The
space holds a varied mix of rehabilitation equipment (a treadmill,
exercise mats, parallel bars, exercise balls), supportive medical
furniture (an exam stool, a wheelchair on standby), and a few small
props for therapy exercises. A physical therapist supervises one or
two patients who are mid-exercise on different equipment. The atmosphere
is bright, open and active — equipment is well spread out with clear
walkways, no clutter.

[interactions]
Touching the therapist should make her explain what each piece of
equipment is used for in general terms. Touching any major equipment
(treadmill, parallel bars, exercise ball, wheelchair) should display a
short description of its purpose. Touching the patient currently
exercising should make the patient mention their progress and how the
therapy is going.

A short story arc: when the player first touches the therapist, she
gestures toward the treadmill (which highlights). Touching the
treadmill afterward should make her walk over to it and demonstrate
proper form. Touching the parallel bars after that should make a
patient walk between them while the therapist watches.
